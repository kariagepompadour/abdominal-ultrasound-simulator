CHANGES MADE (this is a modified, derived work — not the original data):
  liver_vessels.json contains centre lines with radii derived from the BodyParts3D meshes of the
  intrahepatic portal vein branches, hepatic veins and bile ducts. The meshes were deformed
  (affine + B-spline registration) to fit the liver of the SPL Abdominal Atlas CT used by the
  simulator, skeletonised, smoothed and given minimum radii so that they are visible on simulated
  ultrasound. The lines were relabelled with textbook finding names: the transverse part of the
  left portal vein follows the BodyParts3D "Trunk of left portal vein"; the umbilical portion is
  the skeleton edge of the lateral inferior segmental branch that starts at the end of that trunk;
  the right portal vein and its anterior/posterior trunks are approximated with distance rules
  (shared course of the anterior and posterior branches, then the first 2.8 cm). The liver segment
  classes (S1-S8) in ../spl-abdominal-atlas/abdomen_labels.u8.gz are an APPROXIMATION: each liver
  voxel takes the nearest segmental portal branch centre line (S2-S8), the right lobe is split into
  superior/inferior by a horizontal plane at the mean height of the right portal branches, and S1
  is the deformed BodyParts3D caudate lobe. They are not computed perfusion territories. The
  ligamentum teres and common bile duct lines were added by hand and are not from BodyParts3D.
